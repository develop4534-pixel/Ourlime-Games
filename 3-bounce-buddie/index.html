How to Play

Goal: bounce from platform to platform and reach the finish before time runs out.

Solo race: three bots jump through the level too. Beat them to the finish; the race leader and final place are shown on screen.

Touchscreen: tap anywhere on the game to boost. Keep your finger down to brake.

Mouse: click anywhere in the game to boost. Hold the button to brake.

Keyboard: press Space, Up Arrow, or Enter to boost; hold the key to brake. Hold C or the ⚡ charge button in any world for a stronger jump. Press L or the lemon button to throw a lemon and stop another buddy or solo rival for 4 seconds (stars and lemons orbit its head); P or Esc pauses.

World 2: avoid red spikes and spinning saws. Collect rare sky coins for a Golden Leaf reward, use the magnet to attract nearby stars, and hold the ⚡ button (or C) to charge a stronger jump. Your two boosts allow an extra midair jump; holding the normal boost while falling glides you down. Checkpoints, a secret shortcut, a hidden cloud cache, and precision rings add rewards. A hit costs 5 seconds; Limey reappears with brief protection.

Worlds 3–7: reach each finish while avoiding moving hazards. In World 7, bounce on Dark Titan’s head or hit it with lemons to deal damage and defeat the boss to win.

Land on new platforms in a row to build a bounce combo and earn bonus points. Collect the glowing shield in each level to block one hazard hit.

Daily Bounce: collect 8 stars in any levels today to earn 20 bonus Lime. Your progress is saved on this device.

Weekly quests: collect 20 stars, win 3 levels, and make 35 bounces for Lime and the Meteor Halo. Weekly quests reset every Monday.

Sky Rewards: play World 2 to earn Lime, Gems, Sky Chests, and free season-pass XP. The Rewards & Shop screen tracks streaks, missions, achievements, seasonal events, and local records. Spend only earned currency; optional vibration is off by default.

Rewards: equip Comet Trail, Orb Aura, and Star Burst after reaching 45, 90, and 135 stars. Collect five unique Sky Coins to unlock the Golden Leaf.

Land on platforms to bounce automatically. Falling costs time, so aim for the next platform!
