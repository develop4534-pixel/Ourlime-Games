How to Play

Move

←→ or AD on keyboard. On touch screens use the round arrow buttons.

Jump

Space, W or ↑. Hold for a higher jump. Touch: the big orange button.

Collect

Grab coins for points. Find the glowing stars (3 per garden) for big bonuses.

Avoid

Thorns, brambles and bottomless gaps hurt. Crumbling leaf-planks fall soon after you land on them!

Checkpoints

Touch a flower flag to save your spot. If you fall, you restart there.

Finish

Reach the glowing garden gate to complete a level and unlock the next. Esc or P pauses.
