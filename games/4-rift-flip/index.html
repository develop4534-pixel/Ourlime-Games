How to play

Move one step per turn. Collect three sparks, then step onto the cyan exit to escape. Sparks are shared and a new one appears after each pickup, so either player can reach three.

The board flips every three rounds between two layouts. A token standing on a void when the board flips falls back to its start and loses its next turn.

The top strip previews only the first three rows of the next layout.

A match lasts 90 seconds. If time runs out, the player with more sparks wins; a tie goes to the player with the shortest legal path to the exit. If both are tied, the match is a draw. Clean Flip badge: finish without falling.
