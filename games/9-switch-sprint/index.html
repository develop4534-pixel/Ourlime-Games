How to play

Follow

Ignore pink

Pink dashed arrows are bait. Follow cyan only.

The banner above the arrow always names the active rule. Later rounds can mix them. Answer with ← ↑ ↓ → or the on-screen pad before the bar empties. A wrong or late answer costs a life.
